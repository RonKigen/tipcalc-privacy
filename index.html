Privacy Policy for TipCalc

Effective Date: December 2025

TipCalc (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a simple tool that helps users calculate tips quickly and efficiently. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how we handle permissions and data inside the app.

1. Camera Permission Usage

TipCalc requests access to your device’s camera for one purpose only:

  • Scanning QR codes or barcodes on receipts to automatically extract bill amounts.

This feature is optional. You may fully use TipCalc without granting camera access.

Important:

  • No photos or videos are captured or stored.
  • No image data is saved locally.
  • No data is sent to external servers or shared with any third party.
  • The camera activates only when you tap “Scan Receipt”.

2. Data Collection

TipCalc does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All calculations and receipt scans are processed locally on your device.

We do not use:

  • Analytics
  • Tracking tools
  • Advertising IDs
  • Remote servers

3. Children’s Privacy

This app does not target children and collects no data from any user, regardless of age.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
